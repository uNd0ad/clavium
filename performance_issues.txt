2. Real performance finding (fixed in vercel.json): the Vercel function ran in iad1 (US East)
   while the Neon database is in eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) — proven by the response header
   x-vercel-id: fra1::iad1::... Every DB query paid a ~100ms transatlantic round trip.
   Measured on prod: cold start TTFB 3.4s, warm TTFB 0.8–1.1s on every page (all routes are
   server-rendered per request; cache-control: no-store, cf-cache-status: DYNAMIC).
   Fix: "regions": ["fra1"] in vercel.json — takes effect on the NEXT DEPLOY. Expected warm
   TTFB after: ~150–300ms.
